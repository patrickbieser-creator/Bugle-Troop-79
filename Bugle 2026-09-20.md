Troop 79 is hosting a community wide rummage sale to raise money for troop programs, camping equipment, and our many activities. Troop families, friends, neighbors, and members of the community are invited to donate useful items. This is a major troop-wide project, and success depends on everyone helping with donations, promotion, setup, staffing, and cleanup.

A successful sale could help support:
* Camping trips and outdoor activities
* Troop equipment and supplies
* Advancement and leadership programs
* Financial assistance for Scouts
* Future troop events and special opportunities

#### What We Need From Scout Families

Begin setting aside quality items now. Ask relatives, friends, neighbors, and coworkers whether they have items they would like to contribute.  Please focus on items that you would feel comfortable purchasing yourself.

Families may be asked to help transport donations from homes to Northwoods. Scouts and adults with trucks, vans, or trailers will be especially helpful.  Do not collect large furniture or unusually heavy items without first confirming that the troop can accept and transport them.

Sign up here: https://www.troop-79.com/events/110/signup

We will need Scouts and adults to help with:

* Receiving and sorting donations
* Testing and cleaning merchandise
* Pricing items
* Setting up tables and displays
* Staffing sales areas
* Helping customers
* Running checkout stations
* Carrying larger purchases
* Selling food and beverages
* Packing up / tossing out remaining donations
* Cleaning the parking lot after the sale


## Log in to Your Troop Account

All scout and parents. Please log in to your account on the new troop website at https://www.troop-79.com/member. 

No password is required. Once you're in you can:

- Register for events in one click
- Update name, address, phone, email, school information on the Members page
- Submit requests for advancement sign-off. Three scouts have done this so far. They said this new option was very cool. (And not 6-7 at all.) 
- Check your scout account balance and activity (can drive and wreath sale proceeds)
- Submit requests to spend your scout account funds

**Steps to Sign In**

1. Go to [Troop-79.com/member](https://www.troop-79.com/member) and click the sign in button
2. Use the private troop password: *79troop*
3. Start typing your name until you see it pop up on the screen.
4. Pick the name that matches the email we already have on file (If you received this email, we have yours on file.)
5. Check your inbox for an email from Troop 79 with a one-time code. If you don't see it, check your spam folder.
6. Enter the six-digit number into the web page. You will not have to signin for 30 days if you're a scout, 90 days if you're an adult. 
7. If you never want to enter the six digit code again, set up a Passkey. While this step is optional, we highly encourage it. (If you want to understand Passkeys, search YouTube. There are 1,000 videos with people explaining the many advantages. Passkeys let you sign in with your phone, fingerprint, or face instead of a code. It's the same thing your bank already uses.)

Trouble signing in? Text Patrick — or email bsatroop79bg@gmail.com.
