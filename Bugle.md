### Selling Tips and Enter Orders 

There are **three** easy ways for you and your customers to order wreaths. Watch this **5 minute** video or read below (or do both).

[![Your Personal Wreath Website](https://Troop79.b-cdn.net/Wreath-Training-Video.png){.img-600}](https://player.mediadelivery.net/play/781797/0dd1cc18-7819-4f4b-8068-d7844763e30b)

### 1. Your Personal Web Page

Each scout has their own webpage to share with the public. It uses your firstname-lastinitial. Here are a few examples. Your page will track **your orders** and does not charge a delivery fee.

- [troop-79.com/wreaths/anjali-s](https://troop-79.com/wreaths/anjali-s)

Share your link with your family and neighbors.

### 2. Enter Your Orders Through the Website

Login to the Troop website [troop-79.com/member](https://troop-79.com/member) 

Now go back to your personal wreath page (**after you log in**) and you can enter orders directly for anyone who bought over the phone or email. This **only works after you log in**. If you are not logged in, you will not see the manual order options.

One more thing. 

Return to the members page then click on the **Wreath card** on the Members page. You will see all orders in your name, and how much money you have earned. You can set your own sales goal and write a personal message for your personal wreath page.  

[![Wreath Personal Page](https://Troop79.b-cdn.net/WreathPersonal-Page.png){.img-400}](https://troop-79.com/member)


### 3. Use the Public Page

The Troop website is now open to the general public for anyone to order a wreath.

- [troop-79.com/wreaths](https://troop-79.com/wreaths)

When someone purchases a wreath through the public page, they can select your name at checkout so you receive credit for the sale.

Of course, it's better to direct friends, family, and neighbors to your **personal page**. But if they use the public page and know your name, they can still give you credit for their purchase. Otherwise, the credit goes to the troop.

Customers using the public page who order less than $50 will be charged a $5 delivery fee if they choose delivery.  This charge does not appear if they use your personal page.

Please email us ASAP with any questions or confusion.
