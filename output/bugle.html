Greenbush Highlights

 

It was the hottest spring campout yet for Troop 79, with temperatures in the mid-80s in the shade on Saturday. Hiking through the cool woods midday helped shield us from the heat, but after a few miles, when we emerged onto the county highway, the full brunt of the sun hit us. Despite the heat and the epic thunderstorm at 2 a.m., the campout was amazing. Best of all, a half dozen scouts completed their last second class requirements!

Some highlights:

  • Shopping for food at Aldi
  • Navigating cross country through thick and thin with only a map and compass
  • Finding morel mushrooms (Quinn, Ronnie, Patrick)
  • Scrapes on legs and arms from the thicket, but no injuries
  • 83 degrees official high temperature
  • An improvised decision to go swimming at Long Lake
  • Peach cobbler and brownie desserts
  • Epic middle-of-the-night thunderstorm with torrential rain, and no one got wet!
  • Improvising a cover for the carport out of old tarps
  • The old Wade House historical site, goats, a blacksmith shop
  • Cooling our feet in the cold Goose Pond at Greenbush
  • New songs at the campfire
  • Excellent leadership and support from our junior leaders
  • Zip packs in the open fire (and none got burned)
  • Pumping water!
