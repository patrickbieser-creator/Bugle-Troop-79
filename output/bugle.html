New Troop Website - Login to Your Account

 

Take a moment to sign in to the new troop website at https://www.troop-79.com/member.

No password is required. Once you're in, go to the Members page to review your options.

Trouble signing in? Text Patrick - or email bsatroop79bg@gmail.com.

Why setup a login?

  • With an account you can register for events in one click. (Like the Court of Honor next week.)
  • Check your Scout account $$ information and activity.
  • Update profile information: email addresses, home address, school, grade, birthdays, new members of your household.
  • Submit evidence of advancement progress without waiting for a troop meeting
  • Check registration status. (Coming soon)

Steps to Sign In

  1. Go to Troop-79.com/member and click the sign in button
  2. Use the private troop password: 79troop
  3. Start typing your name until you see it pop up on the screen.
  4. Pick the name that matches the email we already have on file (If you received this email, we have yours on file.)
  5. Check your inbox for an email from Troop 79 with a one-time code. If you don't see it, check your spam folder.
  6. Enter the six-digit number into the web page. You will not have to re-login for 30 days if you're a scout, 90 days if you're an adult.
  7. If you never want to enter the six digit code again, set up a Passkey. While this step is optional, we highly encourage it. (If you want to understand Passkeys, search YouTube. There's about 1,000 videos with people explaining the many advantages. Passkeys let you sign in with your phone, fingerprint, or face instead of a code. It's the same thing your bank already uses.)

Troop Rummage Sale is Three Weeks Away

 

Saturday, September 26, 2026, 7:00am - 6:00pm
Uniform: Class A Uniform
Location: Northwoods parking lot
Food: Brat Sale

Troop 79 is hosting a community wide rummage sale to raise money for troop programs, camping equipment, and our many activities. Troop families, friends, neighbors, and members of the community are invited to donate useful items. This is a major troop-wide project, and success depends on everyone helping with donations, promotion, setup, staffing, and cleanup.

Our goal is to raise approximately $5,000 or more for Troop 79.

A successful sale could help support:

  • Camping trips and outdoor activities
  • Troop equipment and supplies
  • Advancement and leadership programs
  • Financial assistance for Scouts
  • Future troop events and special opportunities

The more high-quality donations we receive, and the more people who attend, the more successful the sale will be.

What We Need From Scout Families

Begin setting aside quality items now. Ask relatives, friends, neighbors and coworkers whether they have items they would like to contribute. Please focus on items that you would feel comfortable purchasing yourself.

Families may be asked to help transport donations from homes to Northwoods. Scouts and adults with trucks, vans or trailers will be especially helpful. Do not collect large furniture or unusually heavy items without first confirming that the troop can accept and transport them.

We will need Scouts and adults to help with:

  • Receiving and sorting donations
  • Testing and cleaning merchandise
  • Pricing items
  • Setting up tables and displays
  • Staffing sales areas
  • Helping customers
  • Running checkout stations
  • Carrying larger purchases
  • Selling food and beverages
  • Packing up / tossing out remaining donations
  • Cleaning the parking lot after the sale

Volunteer shifts and specific assignments will be announced before the event. Scouts should expect this to be a hands-on service and fundraising project. Adult supervision will be required throughout the event.


Old Uniforms?

 

It's time to clear out your overgrown closet by donating your used uniform shirts to the Troop. Handing down gear saves families money and keeps useful clothing out of the landfill. Is your Scout growing faster than a campfire? Give their outgrown uniform shirt a second life!

We are collecting gently used uniform shirts today (and at any meeting) to create a hand-me-down closet for new and returning Scouts. Since uniforms can be a significant expense, this swap keeps our program accessible and affordable for everyone. Help your Scout live the value of being thrifty by recycling!
