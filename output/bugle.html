Woodworking Merit Badge (4:15)

This week we begin our focus on Woodworking Merit Badge. Scouts are in for a hands-on, skill-building, and rewarding experience. Each scout will build a personal Stool -- the project that patrols voted for at the meeting two weeks ago.

Our first session this Sunday will serve as an introduction to the craft - we'll cover basic tool safety and proper handling, get familiar with the different types of wood and their uses, and talk through the requirements for the badge so every Scout knows what lies ahead.

Expect a mix of instruction and hands-on time as we start building the foundational skills you'll use throughout the badge. Come ready to learn, ask questions, and get your hands a little sawdusty.

All scouts are strongly encouraged to download the Woodworking Merit Badge Pamphlet for free from the Scouting America Merit Badge Hub to get a head start.

Eagle Merit Badge Option (4:15)

Older scouts who would rather work on Eagle Requirement Merit Badges are welcome to use the meeting time on Sunday to continue (or beginning) this work. Please send a text or email to Patrick to let him know of your plans so we can have resources available as needed.


High Adventure Meeting - This Sunday

 

Sunday, April 19, 2026, 5:45-6:45
Location: Northwoods 1572 E Capitol Drive
Who: Older scouts and one parent of those attending HAT in June

In just a few short months, the older scouts of Troop 79 will travel to the Boundary Waters Canoe Area (BWCA) in northern Minnesota for a week of paddling adventures in the wilderness.

There is still much planning to do to make this trip successful. This Sunday, all scouts, and at least one parent of those scouts, should attend the planning and logistics meeting, which will start right after the regular troop meeting. We will discuss the following items, make decisions, and make assignments.

Trip Dates: June 20-27

  • Assign Crews
  • Plan Routes for each Crew
  • What to Expect
  • Decide on Menus
  • Decide on outfitter gear
  • Set Final Payments Due Date
  • Physicals - is yours up-to-date?
  • Advanced Wilderness First Aid Training Option
  • Personal Gear Checklist
  • Q&A

Maya's Eagle Project - This Wednesday

 

Hi, I am planning to begin work on my Eagle Project this Wednesday, April 22 at 5:30pm. I am repairing a little library and a sign for the Urban Ecology Center.

For the first part, I will need 4 scouts and 2 adults to help with the repairs and painting. If you are able to help please send me an email at mayalynn500@gmail.com

This is Part I of my project. When the repairs are done I will install the library and sign at the UEC (1500 E Park Place) on Sunday morning, May 3. We will dig a hole in the front of the UEC, pour concrete, set posts into the cement, let the cement harden, then later that day attach the sign and library to the posts.

We will have food and snacks both days!

Thanks so very much! Maya Lynn

Troop Treasurer Needed

 

With the recent retirement of Debbie Taylor, the Troop is looking for a new treasurer. We are most grateful for the five years of time, effort, and patience that Debbie Taylor put into being the Troop's founding treasurer. Thank you Debbie!

The duties of the treasurer are:

  • Handles troop funds, manages checking/savings accounts, and prepares an annual budget.
  • Keeps records (receipts, bank statements) and submits monthly and annual financial reports to the Troop committee.
  • Collects camp payments, fundraising income, keeps Troop ledger up to date, pays bills, and manages fundraising finances.
  • Manages scout account income and outflow from can drive, wreath sales, and scout submitted expenses

If you are interested in helping out with all or part of this job, contact Jack Kosmoski at 612-616-1469 or jack.kosmoski@gmail.com
