(4:15) Woodwork Merit Badge Part II

This week we continue work on Woodwork MB. Scouts will learn to handle power tools and begin work on their stools.

Woodwork Merit Badge Project Tracking Sheet

The meeting will be a hands-on, skill-building, and rewarding experience. Each scout will build a personal Stool - the project that patrols voted for at the meeting two weeks ago.

Expect a mix of instruction and hands-on time as we start building the foundational skills you'll use throughout the badge. Come ready to learn, ask questions, and get your hands a little sawdusty.

All scouts are strongly encouraged to download the Woodworking Merit Badge Pamphlet for free from the Scouting America Merit Badge Hub to get a head start.

Maya's Eagle Project Continues Next Weekend

 

First, thank you so very much to everyone who came to help last Wednesday. We accomplished so much and completed much of the repair work and painting.

I am planning to continue work on my Eagle Project next Saturday, May 2, from 3:00-5:30 and Sunday, May 3, from 11:00-2:00. I am repairing a little library and a sign for the Urban Ecology Center. If you can help for even part of the time -- I need about 2 hours each day -- that would be great.

For the next two parts, I will need 4 scouts and 2 adults to help with both the library and kiosk. If you are able to help, please send me an email at mayalynn500@gmail.com

This is Part II and Part III of my project. When the repairs are done, I will install the library and sign at the UEC (1500 E Park Place) on Sunday morning, May 3. We will dig a hole in the front of the UEC, pour concrete, set posts into the cement, let the cement harden, then later that day attach the sign and library to the posts.

We will have food and snacks both days!

Thanks so very much! Maya Lynn


Spring Campout - Greenbush Adventure

 

When: May 15-17, 2026
Packing Trailer: Friday starting at 4:30
Depart: Friday, May 15 at 6:00pm from Northwoods.
Return: Sunday, May 17 around Noon.
Cost: $30

Greenbush is a beautiful group camping area in the Kettle Moraine State Forest Northern Unit, a large forested area known for its picturesque glacial landforms, forested hills, kettle lakes, and prairies, created by two converging ice sheets over 10,000 years ago. It includes diverse habitats like pine and hardwood forests, an extensive natural area, and 100 miles of trails for hiking.

We encourage all scouts and parents to attend this fun campout.

Trip Plan: We will camp at Greenbush Group Campsite 4, about 400' from the Ice Age Trail. After arrival scouts will unpack gear, set up tents, and go to bed! On Saturday morning after breakfast, we will hit the trails, go for a swim if the weather is nice, complete scout requirements in camp, and play games in the huge fields near our campsite. In the evening we will gather firewood for a big campfire. Sunday morning we will cook a relaxing breakfast and then reload and head for home.

Troop Treasurer Needed

 

With the recent retirement of Debbie Taylor, the Troop is looking for a new treasurer. We are most grateful for the five years of time, effort, and patience that Debbie Taylor put into being the Troop's founding treasurer. Thank you Debbie!

The duties of the treasurer are:

  • Handles troop funds, manages checking/savings accounts, and prepares an annual budget.
  • Keeps records (receipts, bank statements) and submits monthly and annual financial reports to the Troop committee.
  • Collects camp payments, fundraising income, keeps Troop ledger up to date, pays bills, and manages fundraising finances.
  • Manages scout account income and outflow from can drive, wreath sales, and scout submitted expenses

If you are interested in helping out with all or part of this job, contact Jack Kosmoski at 612-616-1469 or jack.kosmoski@gmail.com
