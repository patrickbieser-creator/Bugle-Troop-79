May 15, 2026


Spring Campout Starts Today!

 

When: May 15-17, 2026
Where: Greenbush Group Campground on Ice Age Trail
Packing Trailer: Starting at 5:00
Depart: Friday, May 15 at 6:00pm from Northwoods.
Return: Sunday, May 17 around noon.
Cost: $30

Last Minute Update:

It's going to be warm this weekend with a slight chance of rain on Saturday. It will be cool at night. Be Prepared!

Pack:

  • A small backpack for the five mile compass hike
  • Water Bottle with electrolytes
  • Rain Jacket
  • Swim Suit and Towel (We might go swimming at Long Lake after the hike)
  • Bug Spray
  • Sun Screen

Each scout will pack a lunch on Saturday morning from the food we bought last night at Aldi. Each scout will also be issued a compass and topographical map.

Trip Plan: We will camp at Greenbush Group Campsite 4, about 400' from the Ice Age Trail. After arrival scouts will unpack gear, set up tents, and go to bed! On Saturday morning after breakfast, we will take a 5 mile cross country compass hike to find the Old Wade House state historical site.

This hike will complete the five mile compass hike many scouts need for Second Class. The afternoon will include fun, games, and exploring in the huge fields near our campsite. In the evening we will gather firewood for a big campfire and maybe a few rounds of Mafia? Sunday morning we will cook a relaxing breakfast and then reload and head for home.

Greenbush is a beautiful group camping area in the Kettle Moraine State Forest Northern Unit, a large forested area known for its picturesque glacial landforms, forested hills, kettle lakes, and prairies, created by two converging ice sheets over 10,000 years ago. It includes diverse habitats like pine and hardwood forests, an extensive natural area, and 100 miles of trails for hiking.

We encourage all scouts and parents to attend this fun campout.
