High Cliff Campout This Weekend

 

Depart Fri Oct 9, 5:30 from Northwoods
Return Sun Oct 11, 12:00 to Northwoods (help put gear away)
Uniform: Class B Uniform
Signup Signup Here
Cost: $40 per person


High Cliff State Park is a popular destination on the northeast bank of Lake Winnebago. The park features towering cliffs, hiking trails, a large group campsite, and a large beach on the banks of the shallow waters of Lake Winnebago. We will be in Group Campsite 201 and 202.

One of the themes for the weekend will be completing cooking advancement requirements! Patrols will plan, shop, and cook for their meals. We will also explore the many cliffs and towers found in the park. If you've never been to High Cliff, this is a bucket list destination.

Wreath Sale Has Started

 

It's time to sell Christmas wreaths. We're aiming for a great fundraiser this year. Thank you in advance for supporting your scout and the Troop. Should you have any questions, please email Summer Kimble at summer.curtis@yahoo.com and we'll get answers for you.

Important Details

Monday, October 19th

  • First batch of orders due. The wreath vendor needs this for production planning.
  • Orders are DUE. Phone: 414-378-9901 or summer.curtis@yahoo.com. Order submission portal coming soon....
  • Money is due at the same time.

Saturday, November 21

  • Order pickup at Northwoods in the afternoon.

Order Forms

  • Scouts received a hard copy wreath order form at the Court of Honor. An electronic version will be avaiable on the website shortly.
  • We will also create a link to a wreath catalog PDF with photos and descriptions of each wreath.
  • Encourage your scout to set a sales goal of 15 wreaths. This sale has been our biggest fundraiser in the past, and funds the Troop and Scout Accounts for the year.

Money Collection & Payments

  • Scouts can collect money at time of order OR at delivery, we are leaving this up to each family. However, Scout families must pay before orders will be sent to Rose Wreaths.
  • Money is due when orders are submitted on 10/19. Please continue to sell after 10/19 then additional order money will be due before or on 11/14.

  • We do not allow customers to directly pay to Troop Paypal / Venmo account. (This is a nightmare to keep track of.)
  • Your family's payments of cash, check or electronic payment to the Troop's PayPal or Venmo accounts will be accepted. All payments and questions about payments should be directed to Patrick Bieser.
  • We will not include your scout's order to the vendor until Patrick has collected the money

Pick Up & Delivery

  • Please deliver to customers promptly after you pick up your wreaths on Saturday, November 21. Scouts should let customers know when they should expect their product(s) ahead of time.
  • Scouts and their families will be responsible for picking up their orders at Northwoods on November 22 and delivering to customers.
  • Talk to Patrick Bieser (414-915-6423) if you need an alternative pick up date / time. The wreaths will be stored at Northwoods until then.
  • Please reach out to any of the Wreath Fundraising Committee members (Summer Kimble, Kristin Paltzer, Kara Pitt-D'Andrea) with questions.
  • Again, thanks in advance for making this a successful fundraiser for your scout and the Troop!
  • Happy selling!!
