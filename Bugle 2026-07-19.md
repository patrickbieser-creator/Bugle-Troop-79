## Old Uniforms?

![Old Uniform](https://Troop79.b-cdn.net/Used-Scout-Uniform.png){.img-300}

Parts A, B1, and B2 are completed by a parent.

## Fires in the BWCA

The two crews from Troop 79 paddled out of the Boundary Waters about a week before the forest fires began, and we cannot help but feel how fortunate we were to experience the wilderness - its unspoiled lakes and endless pine forests. Our memories will last a lifetime. Watching the news now is a sobering reminder of how quickly conditions can change, and how precious these wild places are. 

Wildfires are a paradox: At first, devastation, but then a catalyst for rebirth. Fire clears away underbrush, opens pine cones that require intense heat to release their seeds, and fills the soil with nutrients. When crises, change, or loss comes into our own lives, remember that like the forest floor after the fire, these difficulties  build resilience and open the door to change.

## Troop HUGE Rummage Sale

**Saturday**, August 29, 2026, 8:00am – 6:00pm  
**Uniform:** Class A Uniform  
**Location:** Northwoods parking lot  
**Food:** Brat Sale

Troop 79 is planning a large community rummage sale to raise money for troop programs, camping, equipment, and activities. We are inviting troop families, friends, neighbors, and members of the community to donate useful items. This will be a major troop-wide project, and its success will depend on everyone helping with donations, promotion, setup, staffing and cleanup.

![Rummage Sale Flyer](https://Troop79.b-cdn.net/articles/6f99821a-f9ef-4a00-b8a1-56814c5ec1bf-rummage-sale-promo-bannner.png){.img-400}

Our goal is to raise approximately **$5,000 or more** for Troop 79.

A successful sale could help support:
* Camping trips and outdoor activities
* Troop equipment and supplies
* Advancement and leadership programs
* Financial assistance for Scouts
* Future troop events and special opportunities

The more high-quality donations we receive, and the more people that attend, the more successful the sale will be.

#### What We Need From Scout Families

Begin setting aside quality items now. Ask relatives, friends, neighbors and coworkers whether they have items they would like to contribute.  Please focus on items that you would feel comfortable purchasing yourself.

Families may be asked to help transport donations from homes to Northwoods. Scouts and adults with trucks, vans or trailers will be especially helpful.  Do not collect large furniture or unusually heavy items without first confirming that the troop can accept and transport them.

We will need Scouts and adults to help with:

* Receiving and sorting donations
* Testing and cleaning merchandise
* Pricing items
* Setting up tables and displays
* Staffing sales areas
* Helping customers
* Running checkout stations
* Carrying larger purchases
* Selling food and beverages
* Packing up / tossing out remaining donations
* Cleaning the parking lot after the sale

Volunteer shifts and specific assignments will be announced before the event.  Scouts should expect this to be a hands-on service and fundraising project. Adult supervision will be required throughout the event.
