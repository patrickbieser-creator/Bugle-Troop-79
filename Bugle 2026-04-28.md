## Spring Campout is 2 Weeks Away

**When:**  May 15-17, 2026  
**Where**  Greenbush Group Campground on Ice Age Trail  
**Packing Trailer:**  Starting at 4:30   
**Depart:**  Friday, May 15 at 6:00pm from Northwoods.  
**Return:** Sunday, May 17 around Noon.   
**Cost:** $30  

[Register for Fall Campout at Pinewoods - Google Form](https://forms.gle/YysNYVYERB3knq6y6)

Greenbush is a beautiful group camping area in the Kettle Moraine State Forest Northern Unit, a large forested area known for its picturesque glacial landforms,
forested hills, kettle lakes, and prairies, created by two converging ice sheets over 10,000 years ago. It includes diverse
habitats like pine and hardwood forests, an extensive natural area, and 100 miles of trails for hiking.

We encourage all scouts and parents to attend this fun campout. 

**Trip Plan**: We will camp at Greenbush Group Campsite 4, about 400’ from the Ice Age Trail. After arrival scouts will
unpack gear, set up tents, and go to bed! On Saturday morning after breakfast, we will hit the trails, complete the **five mile compass hike** many scouts need for *Second Class*, complete scout requirements in camp. In the afternoon will off fun, games, and exploring in the huge fields near our campsite. In the evening we will gather firewood for a big campfire and maybe a few rounds of Mafia?  Sunday morning we will cook a relaxing breakfast and then reload and head for home.
