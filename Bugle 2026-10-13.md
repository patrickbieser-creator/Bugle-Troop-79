# Troop 79 Bugle
**October 13, 2026**


## Important Update for Wreath Sales 

There are now three (3) super easy ways to order wreaths. 

**You have 24 hours to test. Please don't delay.**

### 1. Direct from Your Own Web Page

Each scout now has their own webpage to share with the public. You can share your page (starting Thrusday) with anyone. It uses your firstname-yourlastinitial. Here are a few examples.

- [troop-79.com/wreaths/anjali-s](https://troop-79.com/wreaths/anjali-s)
- [troop-79.com/wreaths/ben-b](https://troop-79.com/wreaths/ben-b)
- [troop-79.com/wreaths/lily-p](https://troop-79.com/wreaths/lily-p)

Try your name now. Test it out. Place several test orders and hit submit. 

All order placed in the next 24 hours will be deleted so don't put in any real orders yet. 

### 2. Login to the Website

Login to the Troop website [troop-79.com/member](https://troop-79.com/member) and then click on the Wreath card on your Members page. You will see all the test orders you just placed, and how much money you earned. You will also have an opportunity to set your own goal and write your own personal message for your personal wreath home page. Make changes to your goals and message and then test your order page again. 

One more thing. 

If you go back to your personal wreath home page **after you log in**, you can also enter orders directly for anyone who bought over the phone or via email. **Enter some test order this way as well**  Remember, this **only works after you log in**. If you are not logged in, you will not see the manual order options.

Please use this method to enter your non-website orders. Again, test it out first. We will delete all orders Wednesday night, and then it will be live.

### 3. Use the Public Page

Starting in two days, we will open the Troop website to the general public and they will see this page.

- [troop-79.com/wreaths](https://troop-79.com/wreaths)

When the public buys a wreath and goes to checkout, they will have the opportunity select your name. Of course, it would be better to direct your friends, family, and neighbors to your personal page, but if you don't, and if they know you, they can give you credit for their purchase. Otherwise, the credit goes to the troop.

Please email us ASAP with any bugs, questions, or confusion. You have 24 hours.
